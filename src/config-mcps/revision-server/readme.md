Editing and Revision MCP - Essential Tools
Book Server (Structure Revision)

get_book - To review the overall book structure and goals
get_chapter / list_chapters - To examine chapter structure and flow
get_scene / list_scenes - To review scene content and pacing
update_scene - To revise scene content, dialogue, descriptions
update_chapter - To update chapter status and make structural changes
reorder_scenes - To adjust scene sequencing for better flow

Writing Server (Validation and Quality)

validate_chapter_structure - To check for structural consistency
validate_beat_placement - To analyze story pacing and emotional beats
check_structure_violations - To identify continuity issues and plot holes
word_count_tracking - To analyze pacing, chapter length distribution
export_manuscript - To generate full or partial manuscript for review

Character Server (Continuity Checking)

check_character_continuity - To identify character inconsistencies between chapters
get_character_timeline - To review character's complete arc
get_character_details - To verify consistent descriptions across scenes
get_characters_who_know - To verify information spread among characters

Plot Server (Arc Coherence)

get_plot_threads - To review active threads and their progression
update_plot_thread - To refine plot development and resolution
get_information_reveals - To review how key information is revealed
get_plot_thread_events - To track plot progression across chapters

Relationship Server (Dynamic Consistency)

get_relationship_arc - To review relationship progression
get_relationship_timeline - To check relationship development consistency
update_relationship_dynamics - To refine relationship milestones

Timeline Server (Chronological Consistency)

list_timeline_events - To verify overall chronology
get_event_mappings - To check how events are presented across chapters
analyze_narrative_structure - To evaluate chronology vs. narrative presentation
check_timeline_consistency - To identify timeline contradictions or impossibilities

World Server (Setting Consistency)

get_locations - To verify consistent location descriptions
get_world_elements - To check magical/technological system consistency
get_organization_details - To verify organizational structure consistency
check_world_consistency - To identify world-building contradictions

Integration Validation Tools

cross_reference_knowledge - To verify character knowledge aligns with plot reveals
validate_location_descriptions - To check location consistency across scenes
verify_information_flow - To ensure information spreads logically among characters
